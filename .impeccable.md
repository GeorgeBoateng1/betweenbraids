## Design Context
Audience: adults and parents seeking braid, weave and cornrow takedowns, including at-home care. The original site supplied by the user establishes these services.
Purpose: clearly compare prices and move to an appointment selection. Preserve all original information and preview behavior.
Brand voice: tactile, reassuring, expressive. User requests premium modern quality comparable to prior dental design and no gratuitous motion.
Direction: cocoa, butter cream, oversized editorial sans, quiet photography and generous whitespace. Bricolage Grotesque display with Manrope body. Reflex fonts rejected: DM Sans, Fraunces, Inter. Font catalog considered: Google Fonts Bricolage Grotesque.
Principles: clear cost before action; caring rather than clinical; retain truthful preview labels; accessible forms and restrained state transitions.

Update: user explicitly wants a much bolder premium feel comparable to Pearl Dental, with richer animation. Add cinematic entrances, scroll choreography, tactile selection feedback, and stronger conversion hierarchy. Keep all facts and preview limits intact.
